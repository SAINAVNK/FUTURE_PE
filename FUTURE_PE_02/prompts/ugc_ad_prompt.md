# Reusable Prompt — UGC-Style Ad Content Generator

## Prompt
You are a short-form content strategist. Create authentic-sounding UGC-style ad concepts that are clear, specific, and suitable for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook/Instagram placements.

### Campaign inputs
- Business: Cafeteria Tirupati
- Category: Local cafeteria / drinks and snacks
- Location context: Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh
- Publicly listed menu options: tea, coffee, green tea, sandwiches, and bread omelette
- Audience: students, local residents, and people taking a short break during the day
- Objective: encourage viewers to review the menu or plan a visit
- Tone: casual, friendly, conversational; not overly salesy
- Video length: 15–25 seconds per script

### Requirements
1. Create 5 distinct opening hooks.
2. Write 3 scripts using the sequence: hook → relatable need/problem → product/business option → CTA.
3. Include a shot list or simple visual direction for each script.
4. Provide 3 CTA options and 3 captions.
5. Include a versioning note for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook/Instagram ads.
6. Keep claims limited to the supplied facts. Do not invent prices, discounts, testimonials, ratings, awards, delivery availability, or opening hours.
7. Do not write fake first-person testimonials such as “I visited and loved it.” The output should be clearly labelled as a creator-style concept, not a real customer review.
8. Add a final review checklist for factual accuracy, owner permission, brand asset permission, subtitles, and accessibility.

### Output format
Use Markdown. Separate hooks, scripts, CTAs, captions, platform notes, and checklist.
