# UGC-Style Ad Content Pack — Cafeteria Tirupati

> **Mock campaign concept for learning and portfolio use.** These are scripted ad ideas, not actual customer testimonials. Confirm the business details and obtain permission before using the business name, logo, or images in public promotional content.

## 1. Hook options
1. “POV: You need a small break between classes.”
2. “Tea, coffee, or a sandwich — what is your break-time pick?”
3. “When the afternoon feels long, make time for a simple snack break.”
4. “A quick idea for your next tea break in Tirupati.”
5. “Your group can't decide what to have? Start with the menu.”

## 2. Script A — Between-classes break
**Length:** 15–20 seconds  
**Format:** Instagram Reel / YouTube Short

**Hook / on-screen text:** “POV: You need a small break between classes.”

**Voiceover:** “Some days, you just need a short pause. Pick a drink, add a snack if you're hungry, and take a moment before getting back to your day. Cafeteria Tirupati's public menu lists options like tea, coffee, and sandwiches. Check the latest menu and visit details before you go.”

**CTA:** “Save this for your next break and check the current menu.”

**Shot ideas:**
- Start with a notebook or bag being set down.
- Cut to a close-up of a cup or menu (use original footage only with permission).
- Show a sandwich or menu card, if currently available.
- End with text: “Check menu and visit details.”

## 3. Script B — Choose your break-time drink
**Length:** 15–20 seconds  
**Format:** Instagram Reel / Facebook/Instagram placement

**Hook / on-screen text:** “Tea or coffee?”

**Voiceover:** “Your break, your choice. Go for tea, choose coffee, or explore another option on the menu. If you want something to eat with it, sandwiches are also listed. Take a look at Cafeteria Tirupati's current menu and plan your stop.”

**CTA:** “Which would you pick — tea or coffee? Check the menu before visiting.”

**Shot ideas:**
- Two text choices appear side by side: “Tea” and “Coffee”.
- Show a simple menu card or permitted product shots.
- Show “Add a snack?” followed by “Sandwich”.
- End with the CTA.

## 4. Script C — A simple snack idea
**Length:** 20–25 seconds  
**Format:** YouTube Short / Instagram Reel

**Hook / on-screen text:** “Need an easy snack idea?”

**Voiceover:** “When you want something simple, check out a menu with drinks and snack options in one place. Cafeteria Tirupati's public listing includes tea, coffee, green tea, sandwiches, and bread omelette. Menu availability can change, so confirm what's available today before you head out.”

**CTA:** “Save the idea and confirm today's menu and opening hours.”

**Shot ideas:**
- Text overlay: “Drink + snack break”.
- Show menu items only if current and available, with business permission.
- Add subtitles to every spoken line.
- End on a clean title card: “Cafeteria Tirupati — check current details before visiting.”

## 5. CTA alternatives
- “Check the current menu before you visit.”
- “Save this idea for your next break.”
- “Confirm today's availability and plan your stop.”

### Caption 2

### Caption 3
A short-form content concept for a local cafeteria: one audience need, multiple hooks, and a clear next step. Built as a Prompt Engineering portfolio project.

## 7. Platform adaptation
- **Instagram Reels:** Use vertical 9:16 framing, bold opening text, concise subtitles, and one CTA.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Keep the first line immediate, make all key information understandable without sound, and finish with a clear action.
- **Facebook/Instagram ads:** Test different hooks separately; avoid unverified offers and use only approved brand assets.

## 8. Quality and permission checklist
- [ ] Confirm that the listed menu items are currently available.
- [ ] Confirm current address, business name, and opening hours.
- [ ] Obtain permission before using logos, interior photos, product photos, or footage of staff/customers.
- [ ] Do not present the scripts as real customer reviews.
- [ ] Do not invent discounts, prices, awards, ratings, or delivery service.
- [ ] Add accurate subtitles and readable on-screen text.
- [ ] Label any mockup clearly as a portfolio concept.
