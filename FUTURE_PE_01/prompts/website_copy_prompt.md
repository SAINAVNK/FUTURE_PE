# Reusable Prompt — AI Website Copy Generator for Local Businesses

## Prompt
Act as an experienced website copywriter for a local Indian business. Write clear, specific, customer-focused website copy that explains what the business offers and gives visitors a useful next step.

### Business inputs
- Business name: Cafeteria Tirupati
- Business category: Cafeteria / tea and snack stop
- City: Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, India
- Verified public-listing details: the public listing describes tea, coffee, milk, green tea, sandwiches, and bread omelette. The listed location is near the Municipal Office in Tirupati.
- Target audience: local residents, students, office workers, and visitors looking for a simple drink or snack
- Primary goal: help a visitor understand the offer and decide whether to view the menu or plan a visit
- Tone: warm, clear, friendly, and natural; avoid exaggerated claims
- Language: simple English

### Instructions
1. Use only the details supplied above as facts.
2. Do not invent awards, customer testimonials, hygiene certifications, popularity claims, discounts, delivery services, exact opening hours, prices, or business policies.
3. If any information is missing, use a bracketed placeholder such as `[confirm opening hours]` rather than guessing.
4. Write copy in short, scannable sections suitable for a small-business website.
5. Make the value proposition clear without promising outcomes.
6. Create these sections:
   - Homepage hero: headline, subheadline, primary CTA, secondary CTA
   - Short introduction
   - Menu/services section with one concise description for each known item
   - Why visit section with restrained, factual wording
   - Location/contact CTA using placeholders for unverified details
   - Three alternative headline options
7. At the end, include a fact-check checklist and identify anything the business owner should confirm before publication.

### Output format
Use Markdown headings and lists. Keep the website copy distinct from your fact-check notes.
