# Research Notes and Assumptions

## Public information consulted
- Public listing: https://cafeteriatirupati.ueniweb.com/
- Listing reviewed for this draft on: 27 October 2026

The public listing describes Cafeteria Tirupati as a cafeteria in Tirupati and lists tea, coffee, milk, green tea, sandwiches, and bread omelette. It lists the location as near the Municipal Office in Tirupati. Public listings can be out of date, so this information must be reconfirmed before actual business use.

## What was not assumed
This project does not claim that the business has approved the copy, that the author visited the business, or that the business provides any unverified service. It does not invent customer reviews, awards, certifications, exact opening hours, or promotional offers.

## Prompt logic
The prompt specifies a business, audience, objective, tone, and facts. It also imposes guardrails against unsupported claims and asks for placeholders when details are missing. This structure is reusable: replace the business inputs, then re-check every factual statement before publishing.
